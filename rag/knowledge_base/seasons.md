# Seasons

- Pre-Monsoon: dry, hottest months, DTWL is typically at its deepest.
- Monsoon: main recharge window, DTWL rises (values fall) sharply.
- Post-Monsoon: recession begins, DTWL partially recovers.
- Non-Monsoon: cold dry months, slow recession.

Each season is modelled separately because the dominant hydrological drivers
differ across them (rainfall lag dominates in Monsoon; ET and soil-moisture
recession dominate in Non-Monsoon).
